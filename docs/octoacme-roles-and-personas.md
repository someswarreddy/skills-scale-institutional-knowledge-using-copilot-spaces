# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder/Client Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Client Representatives define business requirements, provide feedback on deliverables, and approve outcomes. They represent external or internal customer needs and ensure project alignment with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate business requirements and success criteria
- Approve project scope, deliverables, and major decisions
- Provide timely feedback on work in progress
- Remove blockers and escalate business concerns
- Validate that solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain alignment between project objectives and organizational strategy
- Reduce rework through clear requirements and early validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager** for scope clarity, priority discussions, and status updates
- **Works with Product Manager** to refine requirements and validate solutions
- **Provides direction to Developers** through acceptance criteria and feedback
- **Collaborates with Quality Assurance Lead** to define quality acceptance standards

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff meetings and requirement workshops
- Periodic review and feedback sessions
- Change request and approval processes
- Stakeholder steering committee meetings

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and oversee testing strategies, manage quality standards, and ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria before release. They balance quality with delivery speed.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy, test plans, and acceptance criteria
- Oversee testing execution and defect management
- Approve release readiness and manage quality gates
- Mentor QA team members and establish best practices
- Track quality metrics and identify improvement areas

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable deliverables
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Provide confidence in release decisions through clear quality reporting

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager** for schedule and resource coordination
- **Collaborates with Developers** on testability and quality standards
- **Works with Product Manager** to define acceptance criteria
- **Aligns with Stakeholder/Client Representative** on quality expectations

### Typical Communication
- QA status updates and risk reporting
- Test plan reviews and defect tracking
- Release readiness assessments
- Quality metrics and trend analysis

---

## Technical Architect/Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects and Tech Leads make strategic technology decisions, define system design and architecture, and ensure technical feasibility. They mentor developers and provide technical guidance throughout the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, architecture, and design patterns
- Make technology selection and trade-off decisions
- Ensure scalability, security, and maintainability of solutions
- Conduct technical reviews and mentor development team
- Identify and propose mitigation for technical risks

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Enable team velocity through clear technical guidance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager** for feasibility assessment and timeline impact
- **Collaborates with Developers** on design reviews and technical decisions
- **Aligns with Product Manager** on feature technical implications
- **Advises Risk Manager** on technical risks and mitigation strategies

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Technical spike outcomes and feasibility assessments
- Code review and design feedback
- Technical deep-dive sessions and mentoring

---

## Risk Manager/Risk Officer

### Role Summary
Risk Managers identify, assess, and coordinate mitigation for project risks. They maintain risk visibility and escalate issues to ensure proactive risk management throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess potential project and organizational risks
- Maintain and regularly update the risk register
- Develop and coordinate risk mitigation strategies
- Monitor risk indicators and trigger escalations
- Report risk status and trends to leadership

### Goals
- Minimize project and organizational risk exposure
- Enable proactive risk management and quick response
- Maintain executive visibility and confidence in risk management

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager** for escalation and monitoring
- **Collaborates with Technical Architect** on technical risks
- **Advises Stakeholder/Client Representative** on business risks
- **Coordinates with all team members** for continuous risk identification

### Typical Communication
- Risk identification workshops and assessments
- Risk register maintenance and updates
- Risk escalation and mitigation tracking
- Executive risk reporting and dashboards

---

## Change Management Specialist

### Role Summary
Change Management Specialists plan and execute organizational change initiatives, manage stakeholder communications, and track adoption of new processes or systems. They ensure smooth transitions and user acceptance.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate organizational change initiatives
- Develop and execute communication and training strategies
- Manage stakeholder engagement and resistance mitigation
- Track adoption metrics and user feedback
- Coordinate with project team on change readiness activities

### Goals
- Ensure successful adoption and minimize disruption
- Reduce change resistance and accelerate user proficiency
- Maintain organizational alignment and engagement

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Coordinates with Project Manager** for change timeline and resource planning
- **Works with Stakeholder/Client Representative** for business impact assessment
- **Collaborates with all team members** on communication and training needs
- **Partners with Communications Specialist** on messaging strategy

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and stakeholder analysis
- Communications planning and execution
- Training materials and adoption tracking
- Feedback collection and lessons learned

---

## Finance/Budget Owner

### Role Summary
Finance/Budget Owners manage project financial aspects including budgeting, expense tracking, and financial reporting. They ensure financial accountability and provide financial data for project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain project budget
- Track and approve project expenses
- Provide financial forecasts and variance analysis
- Ensure financial compliance and controls
- Report financial metrics to leadership

### Goals
- Maintain budget discipline and financial transparency
- Enable data-driven financial decisions
- Ensure effective use of project resources

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Sponsor** for financial governance and approval
- **Works with Project Manager** for budget planning and tracking
- **Collaborates with all team leads** on resource costs and forecasting
- **Advises Risk Manager** on financial risks

### Typical Communication
- Budget planning and baseline reviews
- Monthly financial reports and variance analysis
- Expense approvals and tracking
- Financial forecasts and resource utilization reports

---

## Communications/Marketing Specialist

### Role Summary
Communications and Marketing Specialists manage internal and external project communications, oversee messaging strategy, and ensure consistent stakeholder engagement and awareness.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute internal and external communications strategy
- Create communications materials and messaging frameworks
- Manage stakeholder communications and engagement
- Track communications effectiveness and feedback
- Coordinate public relations and external messaging

### Goals
- Maintain consistent, clear stakeholder messaging
- Build stakeholder engagement and support
- Protect and enhance organizational reputation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager** to align project narrative and status
- **Collaborates with Stakeholder/Client Representative** on external messaging
- **Partners with Change Management Specialist** on organizational communications
- **Coordinates with all team members** for accurate information sharing

### Typical Communication
- Communications plans and messaging frameworks
- Stakeholder updates and newsletters
- Media relations and external communications
- Feedback and engagement metrics
